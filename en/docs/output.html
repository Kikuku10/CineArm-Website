Output tracks: a motor is not only for focus

A motor is not only for focus — bind anything that turns to a track and it follows the timeline.

Bind it, then control itPick a motor, bind it to a track, and that track is now its responsibility.
Beyond focusZoom ring, mechanical iris, filter wheel: if it turns and calibrates, it can be a track.
Turntables and rigsProduct stages, home-made pushes and lifts — attach a motor and schedule them.
Alongside the cameraMotor tracks and camera moves share one timeline, so the rhythm lines up.
Travel calibrationFor a new mechanism, run it end to end once, confirm both ends, then positions map proportionally.
Several at onceBluetooth takes multiple motors: one pulling focus while another zooms.

Every new mechanism needs recalibration; until then the track shows as not executable — the hardware is fine.
