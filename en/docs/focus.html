Follow focus: program it first, connect the motor later

Program focus like any other track; connect the motor when you are on set.

Its own trackEdit focus without touching a single position.
Exact repeatsFocus positions are saved and can be found again on another stage.
Focus-only keysChange focus at one frame without adding a position.
Ramp itControl how quickly focus travels between values.
Auto-calibrationOne press sweeps the full travel; confirm both ends and take over.
Several motorsOne machine can drive multiple motors, each bound and controlled separately.
