Shortcuts: the frequent moves already have keys

The frequent moves already have keys — and all of them can be changed.

Covered by defaultSave, undo, play, record and audio markers all ship with keys.
Fully rebindableUp to four bindings per command, with conflict warnings.
Portable setupExport your layout for a colleague; no re-setup on another machine.
Keyboard jogPush the arm continuously from the keyboard.
GamepadContinuous control from a pad, with vibration on fault.
End-effector buttonBind a common action to the button on the arm itself.
