Supported devices

Beyond the arm, two things decide what you can actually shoot: the follow-focus motor and the camera. Here is exactly how far support goes today.

Focus motor Sony cameras Other cameras How to verify

1. Focus motor: Air4 wired controllers

Only this family is supported — Air4 over wired Ethernet. Other brands and wireless-only units do not connect yet.

ModelAir4 wired motor. One controller can drive two, shown as Air4 A and Air4 B, bound and scheduled independently.
ConnectionWired Ethernet using Modbus TCP on fixed port 60000; you enter the controller's IP address.
What you needThe controller powered and on the same subnet; a direct cable or a small dedicated switch, not the office network.
Not supportedWireless-only controllers, other brands, and USB-attached motors.

What it does once connected

Focus as a trackFocus sits in the timeline like any camera move; changing focus on one frame does not require a new pose.
Auto calibrationDrive the full travel once, confirm both ends, and the software takes over with proportional mapping.
RepeatableFocus positions are stored and recalled, so a reshoot on another day can match the same focus line.
Status readbackPosition, limits and faults are reported back; problems surface in the run messages with a reason.
Note

If calibration is missing or the motor is not authorised, the focus track shows as not executable — that is not a bug, it means you have not told the software the travel range yet.

2. Sony cameras: USB or WiFi, parameters plus focus drive

Uses Sony's remote control capability; which items you can change depends on the body and firmware.

ConnectionUSB or WiFi. Online devices are listed in the app; over WiFi the camera asks for authorisation on first connect.
PlatformRequires the native macOS or Windows app; Sony camera control is not available on iPad.
ParametersShutter, aperture, ISO and similar exposure settings are editable in the app. Ranges and options come from the body; the app only shows what that body supports.
RecordingStart and stop recording from the app, with recording state shown.
Focus positionDrive lens focus by percentage (0–100%) and animate it on the timeline; focus control must be authorised on the camera.
Live viewThe body's image can come back into the app for framing and monitoring, independent of the arm.
Two caveats

① Not every Sony body supports every feature: older models may record but not expose parameters, or expose parameters but not focus drive. The app reflects the body's actual capability after connecting.

② Focus drive uses the camera's own AF system, so the lens must speak the required protocol; manual cinema lenses normally need a focus motor instead.

3. Other cameras

Not taking over the camera is perfectly fine — the arm only handles motion.

No camera linkPoses, speed and timeline work as usual; focus comes from the focus motor, manually or from a track.
Other brandsNo parameter control or focus drive for other brands in the current version.
MonitoringUse your own monitor or capture card; it does not affect arm operation.

4. How to verify before buying

1. MotorConfirm it is an Air4 wired controller that accepts an IP and port 60000; ask the seller for the model.
2. CameraCheck whether your Sony body supports remote control (USB or WiFi), then whether focus can be driven remotely.
3. PlatformSony camera control needs macOS or Windows; on iPad only the arm side is available.
4. AskPost your body model in the WeChat group and I will tell you what works.
