Supported devices

Beyond the arm, two things decide what you can actually shoot: the follow-focus motor and the camera. Here is exactly how far support goes today.

Focus motor Sony cameras Other cameras How to verify

1. Focus motor: Air4 wired

One family supported. A single controller can drive two motors, shown as A and B in the app.

ConnectingOne network cable from the controller to your computer, enter the controller address once, and it is ready every time after.
How manyTwo per controller — use one, or both, each scheduled independently.
What changesFocus becomes a track you can schedule: when it lands where, set on the timeline like any camera move.
Not supportedWireless-only controllers and other brands do not connect yet.

What you can shoot

Focus follows the subjectAs the subject moves, focus follows — no hand on the focus ring the whole time.
One-tap full travelLet it run from one end to the other once, confirm both ends, and the app knows where every focus position sits.
Repeat on another dayPositions are saved and recalled, so a reshoot days later lands on the same focus line.
Problems are visibleStalled motor, bad connection or missing calibration is reported with a reason instead of a guess.
Note

If the focus track shows as not executable, it usually means calibration has not been done — run the motor through its travel once; the hardware is fine.

2. Sony cameras: set parameters and drive focus from the timeline

Connect with a cable or WiFi and the app takes over.

ConnectingUSB or WiFi; the app lists online cameras and you pick one. Over WiFi the camera asks you to confirm the first time.
SettingsShutter, aperture and ISO are editable in the app. Which items appear depends on your body.
RecordingStart and stop from the app, with live recording state.
FocusYes — focus position can be driven from the timeline, scheduled alongside the arm's motion.
MonitoringThe camera image comes back into the app, so framing and focus sit on one screen.
RequiresA computer (macOS or Windows). On iPad the app controls the arm only, not the Sony camera.
Two honest caveats

① Not every Sony body exposes every feature: older models may record without exposing parameters, or without remote focus. The app shows what the body actually offers.

② Remote focus uses the lens's own AF, so the lens must support it; manual cinema lenses normally need the focus motor instead.

3. Other cameras

You can shoot without linking a camera at all — the arm only handles motion.

No camera linkPoses, speed and timeline as usual; focus comes from the motor, manual or scheduled.
Other brandsOnly Sony remote control is supported today; other brands are left alone.
MonitoringUse your own monitor or capture card; it does not affect the arm.
