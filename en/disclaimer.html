Where I stand

This is not a pitch for DIY. It is simply my own story — why I started with a turnkey kit, and why I ended up sourcing the parts myself.

If what you want is to save time and effort, paying for a complete package is entirely reasonable: hardware, software, setup and support handled for you, ready to shoot. That is worth real money.

My only point: judge whether a service is worth its price before you buy it. Some of it is worth every yuan; some of it leaves you redoing the work anyway — which is the case I ran into myself.
